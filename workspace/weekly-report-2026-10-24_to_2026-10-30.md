## Recommendations — one per platform

1. **Google Ads** — Scale: raise budget ~10% ($8,029 → ~$8,832/wk). The account absorbed
   +49.3% spend with CPA flat (−0.5%), and search product terms is the protected top
   pipeline driver. Guardrail: revert if CPA exceeds $200 for 3 consecutive days.
   → Proposal **prop-846b8fb4** (pending approval).
2. **Meta Ads** — No budget change. Spend was cut −19.2% and efficiency improved (CPA
   −4.0%); the drop in conversions (19 → 16) tracks the spend reduction. Watch one more
   week before drawing conclusions.
3. **Reddit Ads** — Scale modestly: +20% budget ($775 → ~$930/wk) as a low-risk test of
   the account's best CPA ($110.75). Small volume; review after 2 weeks.
   → Proposal **prop-c0b3b28e** (pending approval).
